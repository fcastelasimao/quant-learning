The goal of this research direction is to understand several things about how volume influences
trades.

This is still in its early stages and ideas are welcomed.

First, is how previous high or low volumes can diagnose future floors or ceiling for ETF price.
Or any other correlation between volume in a specific day and ETF behaviour in the future.

Second, the goal is to understand how to scale trading volume we are capable of doing.
Managing a 1k account is significantly different than managing a 1m or 1b account.
How is this done by big comapanies? 
How are orders placed? 
How is slippage handled?
Does the rule that the volume of the order has to be <10% than the total current market volume hold up?
How long do orders take to be fully applied?
At what point do the orders we place influence the market?
